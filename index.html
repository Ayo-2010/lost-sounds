    On the collection

    “The archive does not preserve sound. It preserves the agreement between two people that something was once heard.”

    M. Aldous, Keeper of Records, 1974

    Conservation

    Each specimen is rebuilt from filtered noise, tuned partials and slow envelopes. Nothing is looped from a file, so no two listens are quite identical. Keep a specimen to add it to your private collection; your choices stay in this browser.
